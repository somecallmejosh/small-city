# Small City Studios — business sustainability and website project

The engagement supports Chase in building a sustainable full-time studio business led by mixing and engineering, including remote work, while retaining rap recording and beat production. The website is one part of the commercial and operational work.

Start with the [owner roadmap](./owner-roadmap.md), which defines practical actions, support, review criteria, and an initial 90-day plan.

Use the [sustainability assessment and owner commitments](./sustainability-assessment.md) for the candid conversation about necessary business responsibilities, consequences of leaving them unmet, and practical support for implementation.

Supporting working documents:

- [Business model and service scope](./business-model.md): known figures, revenue/capacity scenarios, proposed scope and pricing practices.
- [Discovery and strategy](./strategy.md): current-site findings, evidence gaps, research, positioning, and search considerations.
- [Service and UX blueprint](./service-blueprint.md): remote/local journeys, offer hierarchy, homepage content structure, and evidence requirements.

Known figures are estimates provided by the user unless otherwise attributed. Proposed prices, service terms, schedules, and policies are not approved public promises. The plan should evolve through direct work with Chase and measured outcomes.
